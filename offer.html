■ Straight Answers

Offer FAQ

How much total will I pay?

Your state's filing fee plus StartBusiness24's service fee: $30 for Formation, or $50 for The Whole Package. The builder above shows the exact math for your state and entity before you pay — the total you review is the total you are charged.

Do you file in my state?

Yes — all 50 states plus DC. A few state fees are still being confirmed; if yours is one of them, no total is shown here and your exact total is locked in at review, before anything is charged.

How fast will my company be formed?

Filing details and timing are set by your state — each state processes filings on its own schedule. We prepare and submit your packet promptly; the state's clock decides the rest, and your confirmation is delivered when the state issues it.

Is an EIN included?

An EIN is free from the IRS — you should never pay a third party for one. After formation we point you to the exact IRS steps in our EIN guide and walk the order with you: form the company first, then apply.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. StartBusiness24 is educational and helps prepare and file your formation documents — it is not legal, tax, or investment advice. For questions about your specific situation, talk to a licensed attorney or tax professional in your state.
