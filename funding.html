Fund Your Business

Your company is formed and your Startup Kit is in hand — the next move is funding it. Through LoanableUSA, a formed company can apply for business credit lines and card-stacking programs of up to $250,000 if you qualify, or for real estate funding for property deals.

  • Business credit lines + card stacking — apply through Loanable's syndicated line of credit program: credit lines and card stacking for the company you just formed.
  • Real estate funding — a separate Loanable application for property purchases and investment deals.
  • Up to $250,000, if you qualify — that number is a ceiling, not a promise: what you are offered, if anything, is decided by your application and eligibility.
