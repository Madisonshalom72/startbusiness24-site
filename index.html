    Get Your EIN — Free From the IRS

    Step 4 of the path. Once the company exists, it needs its own federal tax ID. This is the step owners most often overpay for — so here it is plainly.

    • What it is — your company's federal tax ID: the number the business uses for taxes, so the business stands on its own paperwork.
    • What it unlocks — a business bank account in the company's name, hiring employees, and filing business taxes.
    • When you get it — right after formation. Form the company with the state first, then apply — the IRS expects the entity to already exist.
    • What it costs — nothing. An EIN is free from the IRS, you apply once, and you never pay a third-party site for the number itself.
    Apply Free at IRS.gov

    The IRS's own page says it plainly: beware of websites that charge for an EIN — you never have to pay a fee for one.
