Do I Still Need to File a BOI Report in 2026?

Do I still need to file a BOI report in 2026? If your company was formed in the United States, the answer is no. FinCEN's final rule — effective August 14, 2026 — exempted all U.S. companies from beneficial ownership information (BOI) reporting under the Corporate Transparency Act. The internet just hasn't caught up yet.

What changed, and when

For two years, the standard advice was simple: almost every LLC and corporation had to file a beneficial ownership information report with FinCEN, and old guides warned about steep daily penalties for missing it. That advice is now outdated. FinCEN first issued an interim rule on March 26, 2025 narrowing who had to report, and then made the change permanent with a final rule effective August 14, 2026: companies formed in the United States are exempt from BOI reporting. You can read the current status on FinCEN's own BOI page (fincen.gov/boi) — it is the source that outranks every blog post, including this one.

Who still has to file a BOI report

  • Foreign-formed companies registered to do business in the U.S. The exemption covers U.S.-formed companies. An entity formed under the laws of another country and registered with a state can still have a BOI reporting duty — and even then, the reporting concerns non-U.S. owners.
  • Nobody else. A Pennsylvania LLC, a Delaware C-Corp, a single-member company formed last week — if it was created by filing with a U.S. state, it is exempt under the current rule.

One caution, stated plainly: a multi-state lawsuit filed in October 2026 challenges the rollback. Rules can change in court. This page reflects FinCEN's published rule as of late October 2026 — if the litigation changes anything, we will update this guide, and checking fincen.gov/boi takes two minutes.

Why your search results still say "file now"

Most of the BOI content online was written in 2023 and 2024, when filing was mandatory — and some services still sell paid BOI filing as an add-on at checkout. If a formation company tries to charge you to file a BOI report for a U.S. company in 2026, that is exactly the kind of upsell our EIN guide calls out with the IRS: a government step, dressed up as a fee.
